Request game account deletion

After ownership verification, deletion removes your game account, associated cloud wallet and account data. Unused Gold and consumable packs are permanently lost.

Your Google account is not closed. Deletion does not automatically refund purchases. Restoration of permanent products remains subject to store rules. Career saves are currently local; we cannot remotely erase copies on other devices. Clear app data in Android settings to remove local saves.

How to request deletion

Prepare the request below and send it from your email app, or write directly to support. You do not need to reinstall the game or sign in to request deletion. Player ID is optional. Include the account-associated email and, if known, your Play Games profile name; we may ask for more information to verify ownership. Knowing an email address or player ID alone is not proof of ownership. Never send passwords, one-time codes or card details.

Support address: chepnistudio@outlook.com

This page does not automatically send a request or delete your account. Review and send the draft from your email app.

Timing and retained records

We aim to complete requests within 30 days after verifying ownership. We will tell you if more time is needed. Limited transaction evidence may be retained where necessary to prevent fraud, resolve an open payment/refund dispute or meet legal obligations. If so, we will explain which data is retained, why and for how long. Google and Unity may process their own service records under their policies.
