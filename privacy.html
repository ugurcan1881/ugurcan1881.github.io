Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the data-processing practices of the Football Club Manager mobile game provided by Chepni Studio.

1. Information we collect and process

Game data stored on your device

The game stores career saves, manager and club information, game progress, squads, fixtures, statistics, language and audio preferences, Gold and consumable balances, purchased permanent entitlements, and installed data-pack preferences on your device. This local game data is not sent to Chepni Studio servers.

You may delete local data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This may permanently remove your career saves and other local progress.

Advertising

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising and Google User Messaging Platform to manage consent choices. Through these services, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app launches and ad interactions, diagnostic information, the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, you can make a choice through the consent form and later manage it through the in-game Ad Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Refusing consent does not necessarily prevent non-personalized or limited ads from being shown.

Google Privacy Policy

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4. Chepni Studio does not see or store your payment card or bank-account details; payments are processed by Google Play.

To provide purchase functionality, verify transactions, deliver and restore entitlements, and prevent fraud, Unity IAP may process approximate location, email address, personal or installation identifiers, device and session information, country, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics, and other app-performance data. Google Play also processes transactions under its own terms and privacy policy.

Unity Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy

Data packs and external links

The game may connect to catalog and release files hosted on GitHub to list and download optional community data packs. It may also connect to direct HTTPS data-pack addresses supplied by users. Operators of these services may process IP addresses, request times, and standard device or network logs under their own privacy policies.

Downloaded data packs are stored on your device. The relevant pack creator is responsible for a community pack's content and sources. Review the applicable terms and privacy policy before using an external link or pack.

GitHub Privacy Statement

2. Why information is used

Information is processed to operate the game and save system, preserve preferences, deliver and restore purchased content, display and measure advertising, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse and fraud.

3. Data sharing

Chepni Studio does not sell your local career data. The information described above may be processed by Google, Unity, Google Play, and optional data-pack hosting providers only as necessary to provide their relevant functions. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

4. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Retention periods for information processed by Google and Unity are governed by those providers' policies. Some transaction records may be retained for longer where required by law, security, or purchase-record obligations.

5. Children's privacy

Football Club Manager is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Contact us if you believe such information has been processed.

6. Security

Supported network connections use HTTPS/TLS. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely risk-free.

7. Your rights and choices

  • You can delete local game data through Android app settings.
  • You can manage advertising-consent choices through the in-game Ad Privacy section.
  • You can use your Google Play account for purchase-history and payment options.
  • For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, email chepnistudio@outlook.com.

Depending on your request, we may direct you to the request channels of Google or Unity, which process the relevant information.

8. International data transfers

Providers such as Google, Unity, and GitHub may process information outside the country where you live. These transfers take place under the providers' applicable legal mechanisms and policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game's features, services, or legal requirements change. The current version is published on this page, with its effective date shown above.
