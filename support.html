Requesting support

Include your device model, Android version, game version, selected language, the screen or action where the issue occurred, and a screenshot if possible. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or your Google Play account password.

Purchase issues

If purchased content does not appear, first check your internet connection and use the restore-purchases option in the store screen. If the problem continues, contact us with your Google Play order number. Do not send full payment-card information.

Save and data-pack issues

Career saves are stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game may delete local saves. For a community data-pack issue, include the pack name, version and source link in your message.

Delete your game account

You can request account deletion without reinstalling the game. Sending a request does not instantly delete the account. After ownership verification, the game account and associated cloud data are deleted; remaining Gold and consumable packs are lost. Your Google account is not closed and deletion does not automatically refund purchases.
